Enable local browser control

When you ask an agent to use brw, this extension can read and change content in visible tabs and return the result to a brw daemon on this computer.

  • Handles tab URLs, page content, form values, screenshots, console/network diagnostics and requested downloads. On pages you choose, this may include personal, communication, financial, health, location or authentication information.
  • Connects only to 127.0.0.1 or localhost; Don Works and Revitt do not receive the data.
  • Refuses HttpOnly cookie and bulk-storage CDP methods. Browser control remains visible and can be disabled here at any time.

If you connect brw to a third-party AI service, observations requested by that agent may be sent to that service under its privacy terms. Read the full privacy policy.
